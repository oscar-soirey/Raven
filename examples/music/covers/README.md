# Covers

Square pictures named `01.jpg`, `02.jpg`... up to `24.jpg` (`.png` works too)
are the covers of the 24 albums of the player, in that order; 512 x 512 is
plenty. The colors of each picture become the accent of the interface and the
background while its album plays.

An album with no picture gets a cover drawn by the player in the colors of its
genre, so the example works with this folder empty.

The pictures are not committed (see `.gitignore`): album art belongs to its
authors.
